Frequently Asked Questions
Why does bed bug treatment need two visits?

Bed bug eggs survive the first treatment and hatch roughly one to two weeks later. A single visit almost never clears an infestation for good - the follow-up visit is what actually breaks the cycle, which is why we build it into the programme rather than charging it as an extra.

How much does bed bug treatment cost?

It depends on the number of rooms, furniture involved, and severity of the infestation. We inspect the space first so the quote reflects exactly what needs treating - no guesswork.
