Frequently Asked Questions
How much does termite treatment cost?

Termite treatment is typically priced by the area treated (per square foot) and depends on whether it is pre-construction (during building) or post-construction (existing home), plus the severity of infestation. We inspect first and give you a straightforward, written quote - no obligation.

How long does termite treatment last?

Pre-construction soil treatment generally protects longer than post-construction drill-fill-seal treatment, since it is applied to a wider area before flooring goes down. Either way, KHS backs its termite work with a warranty and offers annual maintenance so reactivation is caught early and re-treated at no extra cost during the warranty period.

Does termite treatment really work, or will they come back?

A correctly done treatment - the right chemical, full coverage, and proper drilling depth - is highly effective. Termites "returning" a few months later almost always means the original treatment was incomplete or diluted, which is exactly why we recommend written details of the chemical and warranty on any quote, including ours.
