Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does pest control cost in Solapur or Mumbai?

Cost depends on the pest, the size of your property, and whether you need a one-time visit or an annual maintenance contract (AMC). We do a free inspection first and give you a clear, written quote before any work begins.

Is an annual maintenance contract (AMC) worth it?

For most homes and almost all businesses, yes. An AMC covers scheduled visits, follow-up treatment and monitoring under one contract - more cost-effective than repeated one-off bookings, and essential if you need audit-ready pest control records.

Are your chemicals and methods safe for children and pets?

We use government-approved chemicals and standard safety procedures on every job, and we'll always tell you what to keep clear and for how long, based on your household.

Do you serve areas outside Solapur city and Mumbai?

Our head office is in Solapur and our branch office is in Mumbai - call or WhatsApp us with your location and we'll confirm coverage for your area.
